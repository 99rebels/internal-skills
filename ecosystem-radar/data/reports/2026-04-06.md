## Summary

**Is the market growing?** Yes, significantly. GitHub stars show consistent growth across all platforms. Enterprise platforms are entering rapidly. Agensi's 18.75% skill growth in 2 days demonstrates marketplace vitality. The shift from development to commercial deployment is well underway.

**Are payments spreading?** Slowly but strategically. Agensi remains the only live consumer marketplace, but enterprise platforms are building payment infrastructure. The focus is currently on enterprise procurement and agency relationships rather than individual skill creator monetization. Payment infrastructure is evolving to support agentic commerce.

**What's the overall direction?** The market is consolidating around enterprise platforms while individual creator monetization remains limited. Major players are building the infrastructure for AI agent commerce, but individual creators like Rian will need to navigate multiple platforms and business models. The next 6 months will likely see enterprise platforms expand to include individual skills as their payment and governance infrastructure matures.

**⚠️ Strategic Implications**: The competitive landscape is intensifying with major tech players entering. Independent creators should focus on:
- Building cross-platform presence (SKILL.md standard helps)
- Developing unique, high-value skills
- Participating in early access programs for new marketplaces
- Monitoring security and trust developments
- Preparing for when enterprise platforms open to individual creators

**🔮 Predictions**: Within 6-12 months, we should see at least one major enterprise platform (likely Google Cloud or Oracle) launch individual skill creator programs with payment infrastructure. Agensi will likely face competition from these larger platforms but maintains first-mover advantage in the consumer space.
