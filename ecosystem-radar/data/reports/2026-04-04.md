## Executive Summary

The agent skill marketplace space is moving faster than expected. Multiple major platforms have launched or are launching marketplaces. Anthropic has committed $100M to a Claude Partner Network and launched the Claude Marketplace for enterprises. Cursor has a live marketplace with featured plugins from major companies (Datadog, Slack, Figma, Linear). The SKILL.md standard published by Anthropic in December 2025 is now supported by OpenClaw, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and Gemini CLI. **This is no longer "early" — it's Phase 1.5.** The question isn't whether a market will form, but which platforms will capture it and when payments go mainstream.

## Summary

**Is the market growing?** Yes, significantly. In the last 3 months: Cursor launched a marketplace, Anthropic launched a marketplace with $100M backing, Monday.com entered the space, and the SKILL.md standard gained broad adoption. Multiple platforms are building the infrastructure for paid skills.

**Are payments spreading?** Slowly. Agensi remains the only open marketplace with live payments. But Anthropic's Claude Marketplace (enterprise), claude skills market (third-party), and Agent 37 (hosted platform) all have payment infrastructure. The shift from "free sharing" to "paid marketplace" is happening at the enterprise level first, then will trickle down.

**What's the overall direction?** Moving fast toward Phase 2. The infrastructure is being built by major players (Anthropic, Cursor, Monday.com). The SKILL.md standard ensures portability. The remaining question is when individual skill creators (like Rian) can participate in paid marketplaces on these platforms. The data suggests this could happen within months, not years.

**⚠️ Risk update:** The competition is intensifying. Cursor's marketplace has enterprise partnerships. Anthropic has $100M behind their ecosystem. Being a small independent creator means competing against well-funded teams. Quality, reputation, and cross-platform presence are more important than ever.
